FAQ

Questions stores ask

What is LocalShoppie?

LocalShoppie is an AI stylist on WhatsApp for local clothing stores. Shoppers scan a QR code in your store, get outfit recommendations from your own stock, and try them on virtually — then order online with delivery.

How does the WhatsApp virtual try-on work?

A shopper scans your LocalShoppie QR code, which opens WhatsApp. They send a photo and LocalShoppie shows how outfits from your collection look on them — no app to install.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Everything runs inside WhatsApp, which your customers already use. They scan a QR code and start chatting.

Does LocalShoppie work for stores across India?

Yes. LocalShoppie works for clothing and fashion stores anywhere in India, and supports English, Telugu and Hindi.

What does a store need to get started?

Just your existing stock and a WhatsApp number. We set up LocalShoppie QR codes for your racks, counter and storefront so footfall turns into WhatsApp sales.
